3. Cross-Sensor Relationships (Exploratory Views)

Scatter and paired plots exploring physiological response alongside vehicle dynamics. Strict exact-timestamp pairing applied without clock interpolation.

Rule Specification
Observations are paired strictly on exact timestamp equality (identical recorded epoch time). No clock-drift offsets, nearest-neighbor matching, interpolation, or resampling are applied. These views are strictly exploratory; no statistical correlations, causal inferences, or stress predictions are made.
