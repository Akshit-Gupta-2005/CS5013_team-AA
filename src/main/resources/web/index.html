3. Cross-Sensor Relationships (Exploratory Views)

Scatter and paired plots exploring physiological response (Heart Rate & GSR) alongside vehicle dynamics. Strict exact-timestamp pairing applied.

Rule Specification
Observations are paired strictly on exact timestamp equality (identical recorded epoch second). No synthetic data generation, nearest-neighbor interpolation, or resampling are applied.
